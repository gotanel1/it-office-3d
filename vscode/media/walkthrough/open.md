## The office panel

The **IT-Office-3D** tab sits next to Terminal. Drag it to the sidebar if you prefer.

- Ten desks: BA, SA, Architect, UX/UI, Dev, DBA, QA, DevOps, Security and a tech writer.
- The PM walks to whoever gets work and hands over a card.
- The big screen on the wall says what is happening now.
- The status bar shows the busy desk, or the tokens used once the task ends.
